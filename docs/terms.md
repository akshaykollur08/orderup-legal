# OrderUp SMS — Terms & Conditions
Last updated: August 19, 2026
OrderUp is a software application that lets users place and manage orders by text message.
By texting the OrderUp phone number, you agree to these terms and consent to receive automated SMS replies.
## The service
You text the OrderUp number to place or check an order.
In response, the application replies with confirmation, order status, or error messages.
The service only responds to messages you initiate.
## Consent to receive messages
Sending a message to the OrderUp number is your opt-in and consent to receive automated reply messages related to your request.
No messages are sent unless you text the service first.
## Opting out and help
Reply STOP at any time to opt out and stop receiving messages.
Reply HELP for assistance.

Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
